The Inference Opportunity

The Shift to Distributed Inference

As AI workloads shift from mega-scale training clusters to distributed, smaller inference facilities, deployment speed and localized risk management become the primary competitive advantages.

The Problem

Over 80% of long-term AI compute demand is moving toward inference. Large centralized data centers face massive grid backlogs, while smaller sub-10MW inference sites face fragmented local permitting, utility delays, and unrated property risks.

The Solution

Ocean Falls de-risks smaller inference sites by sourcing suitable properties, managing local public relations, and structuring specialized risk transfer solutions—allowing operators to deploy inference capacity in months instead of years.

Our Approach

A Capital-Efficient Approach to AI Infrastructure.

We act as a development and risk partner—identifying project bottlenecks, coordinating expertise, supporting insurance placement, and assisting project execution. Every engagement expands our understanding of AI infrastructure risk, building an intelligence base that benefits our partners.

Risk Finance

Insurance-Led Advisory.

Securing commercial insurance for AI data centers is one of the most significant hurdles developers face today. Underwriting these highly specialized, capital-intensive projects requires a deep understanding of technical, operational, and financial risk profiles.

By translating complex physical infrastructure risks—such as power availability, grid capacity, and operational site conditions—into profiles that underwriters trust, Ocean Falls establishes a credible foundation for risk transfer. This capability serves as our starting point, naturally expanding into broader advisory services across the entire project development lifecycle.

Captive Insurance Exploration

To address capacity constraints in traditional commercial markets, Ocean Falls is actively working with industry participants to explore a specialized captive insurance program designed specifically for AI infrastructure. By aggregating risk and intelligence across projects, we aim to structure dedicated risk-finance vehicles that help developers move forward with confidence.
