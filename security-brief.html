The Challenge

Cloud AI Breaks Regulated Compliance

Public cloud AI tools route client files, municipal records, and voice notes to foreign hyperscale servers. For British Columbia public bodies, First Nations, and regulated brokerages, uploading sensitive documents to third-party chatbots creates immediate exposure under BC FIPPA, PIPEDA, OCAP® principles, and the US CLOUD Act.

The Solution

“Pocket-to-Pod” Sovereign AI

Ocean Falls deploys a managed, zero-egress AI architecture that runs locally on your staff’s devices and your dedicated MicroNode—keeping 100% of your data inside your physical and jurisdictional boundary.

3-Tier Pocket-to-Pod Architecture

Architecture Layer How It Works Security & Governance Control
1. On-Device Edge
(The Pocket)		 Field staff run quantized multimodal models (Gemma 4) directly on smartphones/tablets via local browser WebGPU. Zero-Transmission Capture: Photos, gauges, and voice notes process 100% offline in Airplane Mode; zero bytes leave the device.
2. Sovereign Bridge
(The Bridge)		 Encrypted Zero-Trust tunnels and hardware-aware routing connect field devices and office workstations to your MicroNode. Tenant & Role Isolation: Strict authentication, per-user/department access scoping, and automatic failover without public cloud APIs.
3. Local MicroNode
(The Pod)		 Dedicated on-premise or BC-hosted GPU compute runs layout-aware visual document intelligence (RAG) and local fine-tuning. Air-Gapped Compute & Auditability: Customer data never trains external models; optional overnight compute sharing is physically/logically isolated.
